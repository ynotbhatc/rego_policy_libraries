The Largest Open Policy Library: 14,318 Executable Compliance Checks in OPA Rego

The largest open library of executable compliance policy anyone can verify: 14,318 control-level checks across 56 standards (CIS, DISA STIG, NIST, ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA, EU AI Act, NERC-CIP, MAS, APRA, HKMA, DPDP) in OPA Rego, Apache 2.0, with the broadest regulatory coverage of any machine-readable policy library, open or closed.

How big, measured

14,318 control-level checks and 770 policy files at the current release, counted by make count and re-verified by CI on every merge. Compared on 2026-10-12 against every public library we could find — Azure Policy built-ins (5,130 definitions), ComplianceAsCode (2,574 rules), KICS (1,813), Prowler (1,625), the Secure Controls Framework (1,534 catalogue controls, not executable) — this is the largest set of executable compliance checks anyone can verify, and the only one that spans host benchmarks, cloud, SaaS, Kubernetes, IaC, AI governance and regulators on four continents in one policy language. The method and the numbers: docs/COMPARISON.md.

What we do not claim

Largest in the world by raw rule count. Closed commercial libraries do not publish auditable counts and some are probably larger per-setting. We claim what can be checked.
