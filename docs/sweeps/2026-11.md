# Standards sweep: November 2026

**Period:** October to November 2026. **Source:** [`STANDARDS_UPDATE_REGISTRY.md`](../../STANDARDS_UPDATE_REGISTRY.md) v1.1 (every row checked against its watch source on 2026-11-11) and the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md) for release 2.5.0. **Authors:** Tim Coulter with Claude (Anthropic).

Every framework in this library tracks a standard that changes. Once a month we check each upstream, record what moved, and say what the library did about it, or deliberately did not. This is the first post; the next is due in December.

## Act before year end

Three upstream dates fall before 2027 and are not the library's to move.

1. **DPDP (India), Rule 4 on 2026-11-13.** The Consent Manager provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 come into force tomorrow; Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 follow on 2027-05-13. The DPDP module (implemented 2026-10-12) covers the Act and the Rules; nothing in the library changes, but consent-manager registrations are now assessable.
2. **EU AI Act, Article 5 on 2026-12-02.** Two new prohibitions added by the Digital Omnibus (Regulation 2026/1744) apply from that date. The library implemented the amended timeline in #175: Article 50 has been live since 2026-08-02, Annex III high-risk obligations move to 2027-12-02.
3. **SWIFT CSP attestation by 2026-12-31.** The CSCF v2026 attestation window closes at year end: 32 controls, 26 mandatory, with 2.4 Back-Office Data Flow now mandatory. The library module still pins CSCF v2024. Do not use it as a v2026 attestation checklist; the control list has moved and the module has not.

A fourth date applies to UK defence suppliers: the MOD asks for Defence Cyber Certification Level 0 by 2026-12-31 (contractual where DEFCON 658 applies, not statute).

## Modules behind upstream, and why

Four modules are behind. None has an open issue yet.

- **SWIFT CSP:** module v2024; upstream v2026 (current cycle) and v2027 (published mid-2026, no renumbering). The newer catalogue text has not been worked into the module.
- **HITRUST CSF:** module v11.3.0; upstream v11.9.0 (2026-09-24). HITRUST has decommissioned v11.0 to v11.3, so the pinned version is no longer one you can be assessed against. Same cause: the v11.9 text has not been worked in.
- **TISAX:** module ISA 6 (6.0.3); upstream VDA ISA2027 applies to assessments ordered from 2027-01-01, so ISA 6 is still the right catalogue for the rest of this year. Update pending.
- **COBIT:** module 2019. COBIT 7 was announced for 2026-10-27, slipped, and ISACA's page now says "Coming Soon"; a training partner gives 2026-11-19. ISACA says COBIT 7 ships on a subscription platform rather than as a document, so regenerating the module needs a licence decision first (#194). Not yet published, so nothing to regenerate from.

Smaller refreshes noted, not started: NIST SP 800-53 Release 5.2.0 (SA-15(13), SA-24 and SI-2(7) new, SI-7(12) revised, no baseline change); NERC CIP-003-9 and CIP-012-2 now effective and CIP-015-2 approved 2026-08-10, while the module cites CIP-003-8, CIP-012-1 and CIP-015-1; MITRE ATLAS v2026.10 against a v2026.09 crosswalk (metadata only); ScubaGear v2.0.0 against the 104 pinned SCuBA policy IDs; and the CIS Kubernetes, Docker, AWS Foundations, AKS and PostgreSQL directories, which are one release behind the September and November CIS updates.

## Suspended upstream

**CMMC Phase II**, due 2026-11-10, was suspended by the Department of War on 2026-07-13 pending a reform review, with no public outcome at the sweep. Phase 1 self-assessments and SPRS affirmations continue. The module carries phase gates only and is unchanged.

## What the library shipped (release 2.5.0)

- **DISA October 2026 STIG library.** Linux re-stamped and extended: RHEL 9 V2R10 (the 108 mis-keyed IDs recorded in October are fixed), RHEL 8 V2R8 (its last standalone release; RHEL 8 has left the compilation), RHEL 10 V1R2 (new), Ubuntu 22.04 V2R10, Ubuntu 24.04 V1R7 (new), Amazon Linux 2023 V1R5, SLES 15 V2R9. Windows 11 V2R11 and Server 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025 regenerated from the XCCDF; Windows 10 sunset at DISA's final V3R6. Next library: January 2027.
- **CIS.** Windows 10 Enterprise v5.0.0 (501 recommendations) and Windows 11 Enterprise v5.1.0 (535) rebuilt from the workbooks; Apache HTTP Server 2.4 v2.4.0 (all 86 recommendations, replacing a hand-written module with invented section IDs); MCP Server v1.0.0 completed at 55 of 55.
- **New frameworks.** SAP Security Baseline Template v2.6 (ABAP) and SAE AS9100D at clause level.
- **Contract.** Every product framework key resolves at `/v1/data/<key>/main/compliance_report` and fails closed; CIS Controls citations harmonised to v8.1; the PQC module cites the final NIST IR 8547 (2026-10-27).

## Checked, no change

NIST CSF 2.0, SP 800-171 r3, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and 42001:2023, SOC 2, PCI DSS 4.0.1 (v5.0 has no date), HIPAA (final rule not before July 2027), GDPR, NIS2 (four member states referred to the CJEU on 2026-07-08; the directive text is unchanged), EU CRA, DORA, NY DFS, CISA CPG 2.0, FedRAMP 20x, CIS Controls v8.1 and the APAC financial regulators.
